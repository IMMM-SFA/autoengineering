# WIND source attribution

Captured ranges are unchanged portions of the National Renewable Energy Laboratory
WIND Toolkit CONUS 2007 hourly HDF5 object. Source identity, URL and ETag are in
`head-request.json`, `head-result.json` and `metadata-request.json`. JSON metadata
reports are local extractions. The opaque multipart ETag is a consistency pin,
not a cryptographic hash of the complete object.

The [AWS registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/nrel-pds-wtk/) declares
[CC BY 3.0 US](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/). These data terms
remain separate from the package code license. Cite the dataset's recommended
reports and paper listed by the [provider](https://nrel.sitefinity.cloud/hpc/wind-dataset),
including Draxl, Clifton, Hodge and McCaa (2015), The Wind Integration National
Dataset (WIND) Toolkit, Applied Energy 151, 355-366.

No reserved-year object, weather time series or turbine output was acquired. Raw
metadata ranges can include adjacent bytes from the 2007 development object.
